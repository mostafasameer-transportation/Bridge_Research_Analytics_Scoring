Research architecture

  • Observed outcome: keep condition, treatment recommendation, selection/programming outcome, and funding outcome conceptually separate.
  • Condition: use source component ratings rather than an arbitrary average; overall condition is retained as its own field.
  • Traffic exposure: AADT is a candidate consequence/exposure factor, not a universal weight across states.
  • Network consequence: detour, accessibility, reliability, and route importance require additional data and should not be fabricated from the current 14-column files.
  • Risk modifiers: scour/flood vulnerability, fracture-critical status, postings, and similar factors should enter only when supported by source data.
  • Decision governance: engineering review, eligibility, thresholds, funding constraints, and post-score review are distinct from a mathematical score.
  • Optimization: resource-constrained selection belongs after the decision target, benefit, cost, and decision stage are clearly defined.

Current supplied schema: State Name, Structure Number, Owner Agency, NHS Code, Bridge Condition, Deck/Superstructure/Substructure/Culvert Condition Ratings, Structural Evaluation Appraisal, Inventory Rating, Year Built, AADT, and Deck Area.
